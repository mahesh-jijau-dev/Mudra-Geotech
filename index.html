About MRUDA

A Vision for Reliable, Innovative & Practical Geotech Solutions

MRUDA Geotech Consultants, established in 2018, provides Geotechnical Engineering services PAN-India and is committed to delivering reliable, innovative and sustainable solutions for Infrastructure and Civil Engineering projects.

We are specialized in Ground Improvement, Slope Stabilization, Reinforced Soil Walls and Slopes, Geotechnical Investigations, Foundation Engineering, Geosynthetics Applications, Retaining Solutions and much more.
