Our Story

Engineering consultancy backed by technical depth & quality

MRUDA Geotech Consultants, established in 2018, provides Geotechnical Engineering services PAN-India and is committed to delivering reliable, innovative and sustainable solutions for Infrastructure and Civil Engineering projects, specializing in Geotechnical Solutions for Roads and Highway Projects.

Led by Er. Shubhada Jagtap, a Geotechnical Engineer with 20+ years of industry experience, MRUDA offers expert consultancy in Ground Improvement, Slope Stabilization, Reinforced Soil Walls and Slopes, Geotechnical Investigations, Foundation Engineering, Geosynthetics applications and Retaining solutions.
